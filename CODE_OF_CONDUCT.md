# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good faith, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

Report incidents to the maintainers via the contact in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
